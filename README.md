# homelabbing-project
This Github repository documents my homelabbing journey, building up a server rack from the base components,
to a functioning (and hopefully well polished) home networking solution.

My current budget as of writing this (11/25/26) is ~$500 USD

The plan:

Create a completely self sufficient, and well structured, home network, built on top of an old second-hand office PC.
it needs to be:
- compact enough to fit on a shelf, or in a small nook beside my desk
- have enough space to accommodate all

Inspiration for this Project:

I have decided to start this project, as it has been an idea that has laid dormant in the back of my head for YEARS now,
practically BEGGING to be brought to fruition and i finally feel comfortable enough with my technological knowledge,
and i feel that it is at a level where i can begin to understand some of the basic concepts of networking and cybersecurity
that are needed if i intend on going into the field of networking/cybersecurity (which at the time of writing is my current 
future career plan). One of the biggest inspirations for this project are the Youtube channels Lois Rossman, LGR, Networkchuck,
Michael MJD, and one of my biggest inspirations, Linus Tech Tips. (most of whom i began watching when i was around the age of 10 i might add!)

Reasoning and overall purpose of this Project:

The biggest reasoning for me starting this project, is my growing distaste for subscription based services such as:
- Netflix , HBO Max, Spotify, Youtube music, google drive, Microsoft Onedrive, etc.
and many of the other dozens of subscription based services that most people have to employ throughout their lives,
due to either the perceived lack of any viable alternatives, or the growing and incredibly unfortunate monopolization of multiple
types of services, for example, creative tools like the adobe suite having exorbitant price hikes with minimal warning to its users.
I am planning on implementing replacements for as many services as i can, and plan to eventually fully convert all my digital services
over to Free and Open-Source Alternatives.
