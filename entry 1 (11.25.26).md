# homelabbing-project
This Github repository documents my homelabbing journey, building a server rack, 
from ground zero, to a functioning (and hopefully well polished) home networking solution.

My current budget as of writing this (11/25/26) is ~$500 USD

The plan:

Create a completely self sufficient, and well structured, home network, built on top of an old second-hand office PC.
it needs to be:
- compact enough to fit on a shelf, or in a small nook beside my desk
- have enough space to accommodate all 

What i need

Mini pc
- Lenovo ThinkCentre M720q ~$150 (no storage included)
- Dell OptiPlex 5070 Micro ~$140 (no storage included)
- any similarly priced alternatives would also be viable
  as long as they have similar or better hardware specs
- needs to be small enough to fit into planned enclosure
